        Why this matters now

        Disruption is no longer episodic. It is structural.

        Supply chains built only for cost cannot absorb shocks that now arrive every few weeks. They must sense, adapt and recover on their own, at speed. That is the job agentic AI is built for.

        18.7%

        Red Sea and Hormuz

        Share of east-west shipments still transiting Suez, against 80% before the crisis. Rerouting via the Cape adds about 6,000 nautical miles and two to three weeks per voyage.

        20–32%

        Tariff volatility

        Tariffs on China, 18% on India and 25% on countries doing business with Iran. A February 2026 Supreme Court ruling added more uncertainty, so tariff what-ifs must sit inside core planning.

        Fragmented

        Geopolitics

        Russia–Ukraine, US–China chip restrictions and shifting trade blocs push networks from cost-optimised and global to regional and resilience-first.

        Stacked

        Climate and infrastructure

        Extreme weather, Panama Canal drought limits, port congestion and labour strikes compound geopolitical risk, beyond what traditional planning tools were built for.

        Figures as stated in the SAP Hackfest 2026 Theme 1 problem statement.

        Who gets hurt

        When supply chains break, the most vulnerable pay first.

        We chose the medicine case because it turns time into a hard limit: a cold-chain break does not just raise cost, it makes vaccines unusable. The same agents carry over to the other three cases.

        Our focus

        Medicine access

        Last-mile pharma delivery in under-served regions cannot absorb two-to-three-week delays. Nurses, clinic staff and children waiting for doses feel it first.

        Domain pack 2

        Food security

        Kenya and other food-importing nations pay more or switch grain suppliers at a premium when Red Sea routes close.

        Domain pack 3

        Disaster relief

        Relief supply lines must form in hours. The same rerouting agents can coordinate humanitarian logistics.

        Domain pack 4

        Smallholder livelihoods

        A tariff or route change a multinational barely notices can wipe out a farmer's season if no other buyer can be found.

        The system

        Seven agents, one rule: autonomy is earned, not assumed.

        Each agent has one clear role, a boundary it cannot cross and an explicit handoff to a person. The five agents from the brief are joined by two new ones: a Red-Team Agent and a Trust Calibration Agent.

        1

        Disruption Sensing

        Monitors maritime security alerts, weather, tariff announcements, port congestion, AIS positions and news. Classifies each event by severity and supply-chain impact.

        Acts alone when
        Several independent sources agree (credibility ≥ 0.7).
        Hands off when
        A signal is ambiguous, single-sourced or contradicts vessel data.
        SAP Integration Suite · Advanced Event Mesh · AI Core
        2

        Scenario Planning

        Runs what-ifs: Suez closes, tariffs hit 50%, a key supplier goes offline. Scores rerouting, sourcing and inventory options on time, cost, integrity, risk and equity.

        Hard constraint
        Any option that outlasts the Viability Clock is blocked, whatever it costs.
        Hands off
        Ranked options with trade-offs, never a single answer without alternatives.
        SAP Integrated Business Planning · HANA Cloud
        3

        Red-Team NEW

        Tries to break every plan before it runs. Is the signal spoofed? What if the alternate port also closes? Does the backup route cross a new risk zone?

        Power
        Can lower an option's confidence below the autonomy threshold.
        Why it matters
        Stops the system confidently doing the wrong thing.
        SAP AI Core · Generative AI Hub
        4

        Rerouting & Logistics

        Executes the chosen response: adjusts routes, rebalances freight across carriers, updates delivery times and tells downstream partners.

        Fail-safe
        If the transport system is unreachable it escalates, never guesses.
        Hold-and-protect
        While a human decides, it makes reversible bookings so time is not lost.
        SAP Transportation Management · Business Network
        5

        Inventory Rebalancing

        Recalculates safety stock, moves stock between depots and triggers procurement from alternate suppliers within budget and policy guardrails.

        Guardrail
        Never drains a depot below 30% safety stock without approval.
        Equity
        Clinical Priority Index sends scarce doses to outbreak zones first.
        SAP IBP · S/4HANA · Ariba
        6

        Compliance & Audit

        Checks sanctions, trade rules, cold-chain (GDP) rules and import permits. Logs every decision with rationale, timestamps, sources and rejected alternatives.

        Power
        Hard veto over Logistics and Inventory, whatever the savings.
        Output
        Audit-ready report on demand.
        SAP Audit Log · Advanced Track & Trace
        7

        Trust Calibration NEW

        Compares what the agents predicted with what actually happened. After misses it tightens the autonomy threshold so more cases go to people, and loosens it only when trust is re-earned.

        Watches
        ETA error, cost error, false autonomous actions.
        Effect
        The autonomy level is measured, not fixed.
        SAP Analytics Cloud · HANA Cloud
        H

        Human-in-the-loop

        For high-value or irreversible decisions (switching a primary supplier, a route with elevated security risk, exceeding a cost threshold) the system pauses and shows a Decision Card.

        Decision Card
        Options, trade-offs, adjustable weights and a recommendation.
        Reach
        Web, Joule, or SMS and USSD for clinic staff offline.
        SAP Build Process Automation · Joule

        The judgment core

        Two questions decide who acts: how sure are we, and can we undo it?

        Most systems escalate on severity alone. Ours plots every proposed action on confidence and reversibility. Only confident, undoable actions run by themselves.

        CONFIDENCE ↑
        EscalateConfident but irreversible. Example: switch the primary supplier.
        AutonomousConfident and undoable. Example: re-book a reefer truck, bridge stock from a nearby depot.
        Escalate + verifyUnsure and irreversible. Example: act on a spoofed alert with a costly diversion.
        EscalateUnsure but undoable. Example: gather more data, then hold and protect.
        IRREVERSIBLEREVERSIBLE →

        Viability Clock override

        If the remaining cold-chain window is shorter than the expected human response time, the system first takes the safest reversible step, then escalates.

        Hold-and-protect, never idle

        While a person decides, agents pre-book cold rooms and reserve capacity with cancellable bookings, so waiting costs nothing irreversible.

        The threshold moves with evidence

        The Trust Calibration Agent raises the bar after a prediction miss and lowers it only after a run of accurate calls.

        Compliance can always say no

        A veto from the Compliance Agent overrides any saving. Conflicts between agents are settled by rule, not by negotiation.

        Innovation and differentiation

        Everyone will draw the same five boxes. The difference is judgment.

        Six additions that answer real failure modes of a vaccine cold chain, each buildable on SAP BTP.

        01

        Viability Clock

        Every consignment carries a digital-twin cold-chain window. Each route option burns it down; no plan may spend more than is left.

        02

        Red-Team pre-mortem

        An adversarial agent attacks each plan first and lowers confidence when it finds a weak point.

        03

        Hold-and-protect

        Reversible bookings keep options open while a person decides. The system never sits idle.

        04

        Last-mile human mesh

        Clinic staff and drivers report and approve by SMS, USSD or WhatsApp, offline-first. People act as sensors as well as approvers.

        05

        Equity-weighted allocation

        A Clinical Priority Index sends scarce doses to outbreak zones and clinics with working fridges, not just the cheapest lane.

        06

        Disruption memory

        Past disruptions and their outcomes become searchable playbooks, so each first response is better than the last.

        Robustness

        The questions judges will ask, answered in the design.

        Each answer is either shown in the simulation above or built into the rules the agents follow.

        What stops the agent confidently doing the wrong thing?

        The confidence × reversibility gate plus the Red-Team pre-mortem. Irreversible actions always go to a person, however confident the model is.

        Shown in: Red Sea closure, step 5

        What if a signal is fake or wrong?

        Every source gets a credibility score. A single low-credibility source can never cross the autonomy threshold, and signals that contradict vessel data are quarantined.

        Shown in: Spoofed port alert

        What if two agents disagree?

        An explicit rule settles it: Compliance holds a hard veto over Logistics and Inventory, whatever the savings.

        Shown in: audit log, Compliance entries

        What if SAP TM or a carrier system is down?

        Unreachable integrations always escalate to a person. The agent never assumes a booking went through.

        Built into the Rerouting & Logistics Agent

        What if autonomy quietly gets worse over time?

        The Trust Calibration Agent compares predicted and actual outcomes and tightens the threshold on drift.

        Shown in: the threshold slider after each run

        Who is accountable for an autonomous call?

        The audit log records the action, rationale, sources, timestamp and every rejected alternative, so each call can be defended.

        Shown in: counterfactual audit log

        Does cost dominate every answer?

        No. Five weights (time, cost, integrity, risk, equity) are visible and adjustable. Options that break the cold-chain window are blocked regardless of cost.

        Shown in: priority weights

        Will it work with poor connectivity at the last mile?

        Clinic staff approve and report by SMS or USSD. If a message cannot be confirmed, the case escalates. No patient data is used, only consignment, route and facility data.

        Pilot scope

        Does it act when nothing needs doing?

        No. In the Hormuz scenario it confirms none of our lots are exposed, raises a watch level and logs why it chose not to act.

        Shown in: Strait of Hormuz closed

        Is this feasible for a student team?

        Yes. The prototype needs public feeds, simulated shipments and standard SAP BTP services. Four agents run end to end in Round 2.

        Business model

        Customers: national medical stores, donor-funded immunisation programmes, cold-chain logistics providers. Value: fewer losses, faster safe decisions, audit-ready compliance. Revenue: per-corridor subscription plus an outcome fee on doses saved.

        Scales by swapping domain packs

        The same seven agents run insulin, blood, food supply in disaster-prone regions, or auto parts under tariff shocks. Only the rules, weights and data feeds change.
